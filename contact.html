Questions we hear before every booking

Cannot find your answer? Send us a message and we will help.

How early should we contact you?

For conferences and galas, four to six months ahead gives the best venue choice. For smaller meetings, four weeks is usually enough.

Do you work outside Chennai and Coimbatore?

Yes. We have produced events in over 40 cities and manage travel, venues and local vendors wherever you need them.

How is pricing decided?

Every proposal lists each cost line by line: venue, production, catering and staff. You approve the budget before we book anything.

Can you run hybrid or virtual events?

Yes. We provide live streaming, virtual attendee tools and a broadcast crew for hybrid summits and launches.

Prefer social over a form?

Message us on WhatsApp or LinkedIn and a project lead will pick it up the same working day.
