From a five-person team to a national event partner

We started in 2011 with one goal: make company events run smoothly. Our first client had a 60-person sales meeting and a tight budget. Today we produce summits for thousands of delegates.

Every event still follows the same rule. Plan early, share the schedule with everyone, and keep a named person on site to solve problems before guests notice them.
