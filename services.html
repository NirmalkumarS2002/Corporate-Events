How a project runs from brief to review

The same four stages apply whether your event has 50 guests or 5,000.

1

Brief

One working session to agree goals, audience, budget and dates.

2

Plan

You receive venue options, a programme outline and a costed proposal.

3

Produce

We build, rehearse and run the event with a named on-site crew.

4

Review

A report covers attendance, feedback and ideas for your next event.

Common questions about our services

Need more detail? Send us a message and a project lead will reply within 48 hours.

Can we hire you for just one part of an event?

Yes. Many clients book only production, registration or travel. We quote each service separately.

Do you handle venue and vendor payments?

We negotiate on your behalf and you approve each contract. Payments follow a schedule agreed in the proposal.

What happens if our plans change?

Changes are common. We revise the schedule and budget together and confirm every update in writing.

Do you provide a backup plan?

Every event has a risk plan covering weather, power, speaker delays and vendor no-shows.

Ready to plan your next corporate event?

Share your date, headcount and goals. We will send a costed proposal within 48 hours.
